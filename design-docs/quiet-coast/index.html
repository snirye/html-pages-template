The Quiet Coast

A morning on the headland, where the road ends and the tide sets the pace.

Design brief

This editorial layout pairs an illustrated lead with a narrow reading column. The warm palette and simple coastline shapes set the scene, while the serif typography gives the story room to breathe.

The story

The first thing you hear from the path is the water moving through the stones. From higher up it sounds like wind in the grass. At the foot of the hill, you can hear each pebble turn.

At the waterline

We left our shoes above the tide mark and followed a seam of wet sand around the headland. There were two fishing boats on the horizon, far enough away that the wakes reached us long after the boats had passed.

By the time we turned back, sunlight had reached the bench at the bend. We sat there until the last boat disappeared.
